## Executive Summary
HavenHunt is a user-centric AI assistant designed to simplify the property search process for renters and buyers in Chicago. By leveraging advanced semantic search capabilities and personalized recommendations, HavenHunt addresses key user pain points such as information overload and trust issues in real estate transactions. The standout result from our efforts is the successful integration of a Telegram chatbot that can efficiently handle user queries about property listings.
